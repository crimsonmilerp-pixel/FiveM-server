# rp_vehicles

- **Speed limits by vehicle class** (`config.lua` → `speedCapsMph`). Police and other emergency vehicles are capped at 125 mph and get a small boost, so they match or slightly beat most cars citizens and criminals drive. Only supercars (130) are a touch faster.
- **Blocked vehicles:** 152 weaponized, armored, military, arena, and sci-fi models can never exist in the city. They aren't in dealerships, traffic, or the military base, and nobody can spawn them.
- Change the numbers in `config.lua`, upload it, then type `restart rp_vehicles` in the txAdmin Live Console.
